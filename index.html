How CampusResolve works

A controlled workflow: AI proposes, application policy validates, people approve.

01
✳

Understand

Gemini can extract supported facts from the report and provide a structured triage recommendation. The browser prototype falls back to transparent keyword rules when no backend is configured.

02
⌕

Investigate

Search similar reports and group repeat incidents by location and category. Candidates are evidence to review—not an automatic merge decision.

03
⚖

Control

Deterministic application logic validates inputs, applies routing policies and restricts workflow transitions. The model cannot grant itself permissions.

04
✓

Verify

Staff document progress and resolution evidence. The workflow supports review, closure and reopening rather than trusting an AI-generated claim that a fix happened.

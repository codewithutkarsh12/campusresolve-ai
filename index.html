ABOUT OUR PURPOSE

Better campus operations start with better evidence.

CampusResolve AI is a prototype for turning campus facility reports into clearer, reviewable work.

CAMPUS INCIDENT INTELLIGENCE

From scattered reports to accountable resolution.

CampusResolve brings incident intake, advisory triage, recurring-pattern review and resolution verification into one operator workspace. It is designed to help people make better-informed decisions—not to replace campus staff or emergency services.

Human-reviewedEvidence-ledSafety-awareTransparent demo

Our mission

Make campus maintenance reports easier to understand, prioritize and follow through with a visible record of the work.

Our principles

Keep people accountable, show uncertainty, protect sensitive information and distinguish recommendations from verified outcomes.

Our boundary

This repository is an evaluation prototype. It is not a live campus service, emergency channel or secure multi-user incident platform.

WORKSPACE GUIDE FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why this prototype exists, how each workspace tool works, and what its data and AI can—and cannot—do.

Start here: what is CampusResolve?

CampusResolve AI is a campus incident-intelligence prototype. It demonstrates one traceable workflow: capture a facilities issue, review an advisory triage, look for similar reports, move the work through a status flow, and record a local verification timeline. The sample workspace is for evaluation—not live campus operations.

Why was CampusResolve AI created?

It was created to demonstrate how scattered campus-maintenance reports could be handled in one clearer workflow instead of being lost across informal messages or disconnected follow-ups. The prototype brings reporting, structured triage, recurring-pattern review, work-status tracking and resolution notes into one operator workspace. The goal is better visibility and accountable follow-through—not replacing facilities staff or claiming that a real campus deployment already exists.

What problem is this website trying to solve?

Campus facilities issues can be difficult to compare, prioritize and follow through when the original report, location, urgency, assigned work and resolution evidence are separated. CampusResolve models a single place to review those steps, surface missing information and make the next action easier to understand. It is a workflow demonstration, not proof that an institution currently uses it.

Who is the intended user?

The interface is designed around a campus facilities operator or reviewer who needs to inspect incoming issues, consider a triage suggestion, track progress and review the history. Students and staff are represented as report sources in the sample workflow; this prototype is not an enabled public reporting service.

What does each Workspace section do?

Overview summarizes the synthetic demo queue. Incidents lets you create, search, filter, inspect and export sample reports. AI triage produces an advisory category, urgency and evidence summary. Recurring faults groups similar location/category patterns for human review. Audit trail shows the local demo event timeline. FAQ explains the product purpose and prototype boundaries. These sections demonstrate a connected workflow; their sample counts are not live campus metrics.

Why does the workspace say NMIET Campus?

NMIET Campus is the configured demo-workspace label used in this prototype. The repository does not document a live connection to NMIET systems, an official institutional deployment, or an endorsement. The label must not be read as proof of integration.

Are the incidents, charts and audit events real?

No. The bundled records are synthetic examples stored in this browser's localStorage. They are included to make the workflow testable and understandable. Do not present their counts, locations, trends or events as verified campus statistics or real reports.

Does AI make the final decision or resolve an incident?

No. Gemini output, when configured, is advisory. The application validates the response and applies deterministic safety-priority rules, but a person must review the evidence, decide what action is appropriate and verify any claimed resolution. The AI does not dispatch staff or independently close real incidents.

What happens when Gemini is not configured or unavailable?

The interface uses a clearly labelled deterministic, rules-based demo fallback. That fallback is not a live model response and must not be treated as an operational risk assessment. The rest of the sample workflow can still be explored.

What information is sent to Gemini?

Only when the deployment owner configures the server-side Gemini integration and a user runs triage, the report description and supplied location are sent by the server to Google's Gemini API for analysis. The API key stays server-side. Do not submit personal, confidential, medical or otherwise sensitive information.

Where are incident records stored?

Demo incident and audit records are stored in this browser's localStorage. They are not shared between users, server-backed or tamper-resistant, and can be changed or cleared by the browser user. The saved appearance preference is stored separately from the demo incident records.

How does recurring-fault detection work?

The prototype groups reports using normalized location and category information to surface possible repeat patterns. A match is a prompt for a person to investigate; it does not prove a common cause, confirm that a fault is recurring, or establish a safety risk.

Is the audit trail an official or tamper-proof record?

No. It is a browser-local demo timeline intended to show the kinds of events an accountable workflow might record. It can be edited or cleared with browser storage and is not an authoritative, immutable or shared audit log.

Can I export the incident list?

Yes. The Incidents view offers CSV export of the current synthetic demo records in this browser. The export does not turn those records into verified operational data.

Can I enter real student or safety information?

No. Use synthetic examples only. Do not enter student identities, private contact details, medical information, confidential descriptions, photographs, credentials or sensitive incident evidence.

Is authentication and staff access control enabled?

No. Production authentication, server-enforced staff roles, shared persistent records, access-controlled evidence storage and tamper-resistant audit storage are not implemented in this prototype.

Can this prototype be used by a real college today?

Not safely as an operational reporting system. Before real use, it needs a reviewed threat model, authentication, server-side authorization on every protected action, secure shared storage, access-controlled evidence handling, global abuse controls, monitoring, backups, retention policies and an approved privacy process. Those capabilities must be implemented and tested—not assumed from the demo UI.

Does CampusResolve replace emergency services?

No. It is not an emergency reporting or response channel. For an immediate threat, follow your institution's official emergency procedures and contact the appropriate local emergency service.

How can I report a bug or suggest an improvement?

Use the public GitHub issue tracker linked from Contact. It is the available project-feedback channel because this prototype has no connected support inbox or message-submission backend. Never include personal or confidential information in a public issue.

    Create a sample incident

    Choose New report, enter a synthetic title, description and location, then submit it for triage review.

TRUST PRIVACY NOTES

Privacy and data handling

A plain-language description of how this prototype handles demo information.

Use synthetic information only

CampusResolve AI is an evaluation prototype. Do not enter real student records, personal identifiers, private contact details, medical information, confidential reports, credentials or sensitive photographs.

Browser storage

Incident records and the local demo audit timeline are stored in localStorage in the current browser. This storage is not a secure shared database, is not tamper-resistant and may be cleared by the browser user.

Optional AI processing

If a deployment owner configures the server-side Gemini integration, the report description and user-provided location submitted for triage are sent by the server to Google's Gemini API for analysis. Do not submit sensitive or identifying information.

What is not implemented

This prototype does not implement production authentication, server-enforced authorization, shared persistent records, secure evidence storage, a formal retention workflow or a privacy-request portal.

Questions

Use the public project issue tracker for non-sensitive questions. Do not post personal or confidential data in public issues.

TRUST USE NOTES

Prototype terms and limitations

Important boundaries for evaluating this software.

Evaluation use only

This repository is a demonstration of a campus incident workflow. It is not a production campus-management service, emergency-response channel or source of authoritative operational records.

No emergency guarantee

Do not rely on this prototype to report an emergency, dispatch responders, guarantee a repair or confirm that a hazard has been resolved. Follow your institution's official emergency and facilities procedures.

AI output is advisory

AI and rules-based triage results can be incomplete or wrong. Review the underlying report and evidence. Human operators remain responsible for decisions and verification.

Data restrictions

Use synthetic information only. Do not upload or enter personal, confidential, regulated or security-sensitive information.

Production readiness

Before real operational use, the system needs an approved privacy and threat model, authentication, server-side authorization, persistent storage controls, audit integrity, monitoring, backups and operational testing.
